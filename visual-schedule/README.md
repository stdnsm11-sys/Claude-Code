# Step by Step Schedule

A picture schedule that walks a 4-year-old through the whole day, from waking up to bedtime, at home and at school. It works like an AAC board or a Boardmaker strip. Open `index.html` in any browser on a tablet, phone, Chromebook or smartboard. Nothing to install.

## How the day is laid out
- **Four tabs: 🌅 Morning, ☀️ Noon, 🌤️ Afternoon, 🌙 Night.** The app opens on the tab that matches the clock (a **Now** tag marks it). The page colors change with the time of day: peach sunrise, bright noon, soft afternoon, dark blue night.
- Under the tabs, the routines for that part of the day sit on a **trail of round stops**. Each stop shows how many steps are done. Done routines get a green check, and the trail fills in green.
- Under the trail is the current routine as **picture tiles**.

| Part of day | Routines |
|---|---|
| 🌅 Morning | Wake up · Breakfast · Off to school · Arriving at school |
| ☀️ Noon | Bathroom · Lunch · Recess · Rest time |
| 🌤️ Afternoon | Going home · Snack · Play time · Going to the store |
| 🌙 Night | Dinner (food first, then treat) · Bath time · Bedtime |

### School day / Weekend
The **🏫 School day / 🏡 Weekend** switch under the greeting changes which routines show. It switches to Weekend on Saturday and Sunday by itself (you can turn this off). Weekends leave out every school routine:

| Part of day | Weekend routines |
|---|---|
| 🌅 Morning | Wake up · Breakfast · Play at home |
| ☀️ Noon | Potty time · Lunch at home · Nap time |
| 🌤️ Afternoon | Snack · Play time · Going to the store · Park |
| 🌙 Night | Dinner · Bath time · Bedtime |

In Parent mode, **Shows on** sets any routine to school days, weekends, or both.

## Moving through the day
- On a fresh routine, a **First up** banner shows what's first. **Let's start** says "Good morning! First, wake up…" (speech needs one tap first on most tablets).
- **Tapping a tile** says the step out loud and changes the outline: **red** (not yet) → **yellow** (waiting for the child) → **green** (done).
- When every tile in a routine is green, a celebration shows **what's next**. **Let's go** slides straight into the next routine. After the last routine of the morning it moves on to Noon, and so on until "All done for today! Good night."
- **Stay here** closes the celebration. A **Next** button under the board still points the way.
- **First / Then** shows only two big tiles, the step now and the step after.
- **Read it** reads the whole routine aloud.
- Every new day starts fresh, with all tiles back to red. You can turn this off.

## Breaking a tile into smaller steps
**Press and hold any tile** (or right-click it) for its menu. Two choices:
- **📋 Break it down into *Wake up*** (first button): the small steps go straight into the routine as their own tiles, in place of the big tile. One long-press, one tap. **Undo** puts it back.
- **🧩 Break it down, keep it as one tile:** the small steps stay inside the big tile and open when it is tapped.

Already kept inside a tile? Open it and tap **📋 Put these steps in *Wake up*** next to the Back button, or long-press the big tile → **📋 Put the steps in…**. Long-press one small step → **⬆️ Add this step to *Wake up*** copies just that step into the routine, right after the big tile.

The details for the "keep it as one tile" choice: For example, *Get dressed* becomes *Go to dresser, Open drawer, Underwear on, Pants on, Shirt on, Socks on*.

- The board steps **into** the tile and shows its small steps. A **⬆ Back** button and a path (*Wake up › Get dressed*) show where you are.
- **Small steps can be broken down again.** Hold *Shirt on* and it becomes *Get shirt, Find the tag, Head in, One arm in, Other arm in, Pull it down*.
- A broken-down tile looks like a stack of cards with a **🧩 steps** chip. Tapping it opens its small steps. Its color comes from them: yellow once one is started, green when all are done.
- When the last small step turns green, the app says "Get dressed. All done!" and climbs back up to the bigger step. When a whole routine is done, the usual celebration shows what's next.
- The menu can also **break it down a different way** (Claude), **put it back together** (with Undo), say the step, and in Parent mode edit it or **add my own smaller steps**.

**Step size follows the child's developmental level.** Pick it in the menu or in Parent mode settings. Choose by the child's skills, not only age:

| Level | About | Steps | Words per tile |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early | 2–3 years | 2–3 | 1–2 |
| Preschool | 3–4 years | 3–4 | 2–3 |
| Pre-K (default) | 4–5 years | 4–6 | 2–4 |
| Kindergarten | 5–6 years | 5–7 | up to 5 |

Ready-made breakdowns work offline for: get dressed, shirt on, pants on, socks on, shoes on, coat on, wash hands, brush teeth, potty / bathroom, bath, pajamas on, toys away / clean up, throw away trash, hang up backpack, car seat, line up, and eat my food. For any other tile, Claude writes the small steps at the chosen level (on claude.ai only).

## 🚀 Rocket launch
When the child finishes a routine, a big rocket blasts across the screen with a flame-and-smoke trail, a burst of colored stars, a whoosh-and-chime sound, and "Three, two, one, blast off!" Finishing a whole part of the day sends 2 rockets; finishing the whole day sends 3. Then the celebration shows what's next. Turn it off in Parent mode settings. With the device's "reduce motion" setting on, it skips the animation and just says "Blast off!"

## Printing
Tap **🖨️ Print** at the top. Choose:
- **What to print:** this routine (or the small steps you're in), the whole part of the day, or the whole day (school day or weekend, whichever is showing).
- **Card size:** Large (2 across), Medium (3 across) or Small (4 across).
- **Add the small steps** of broken-down tiles as their own numbered sections.
- **Dotted cut lines** for cutting cards out for velcro strips or a first/then board.

A preview shows the first page. **Save PDF to print** makes a US Letter PDF with black-outlined, numbered cards (photos included). Open the PDF and print it. The app can't open the printer dialog itself on claude.ai, so it always goes through a PDF.

### Canon Ivy 2 mini photo printer
In the print window, choose **Printer → Canon Ivy 2 (2×3 in.)**. The Ivy 2 prints 2×3 inch ZINK sticker paper from pictures in your photo library, so the app makes one picture per sticker (1200×1800, 600 dpi) instead of a PDF.
- **1 big card** per sticker: a large picture and big word, with a safe edge so the outline isn't trimmed.
- **2 cards** per sticker: two smaller cards stacked, with a dotted line to cut them apart.
- Options: step numbers, a title sticker before each routine, and small steps of broken-down tiles.
- **Save all stickers (ZIP)**, or tap one sticker thumbnail to save just that picture.
- On iPhone/iPad: open the ZIP in the Files app, select the pictures, **Save to Photos**, then pick them in the **Canon Mini Print** app and print.

## Real photos from the camera
A photo of the real thing (for *Snack*, his bag of M&Ms) shows big on the tile, with the symbol kept small in the corner.
- In Parent mode, tap **📷** under a tile to open the camera right away. The photo is added to that tile, with **Undo**.
- Or long-press a tile → **📷 Add a photo**, or open **✏️ Edit** → **Take a photo** / **Choose a photo** / **Remove photo**.
- On phones and tablets, the camera opens (some devices first ask camera or photo library). On a computer it opens a file picker.
- Photos are cropped square and saved only on this device.

## Parent mode (press and hold **🔒 Parent**)
Parent mode opens with a press-and-hold so a quick tap from the child won't open it. A dark bar shows while it is on; **Done** closes it.

**Changing tiles**, right on the board:
- **✏️ Edit:** change the word, what it says out loud (with a **Listen** button), and the picture.
- **🔄 New picture:** Claude draws a new symbol for that tile right on the board. Tap again for another try, or **Undo** to go back.
- **🗑 Remove**, with **Undo**.
- **◀ ▶** move a tile earlier or later.
- **+ Add a tile** at the end of the routine opens the tile editor.

**The tile editor** gives three ways to get a picture:
1. **✨ Draw it** asks Claude for a simple AAC-style symbol. **Try again** makes another attempt. Every attempt stays as a thumbnail so you can pick the best one.
2. **Pick a picture** from emoji that match the word.
3. **Use a photo** from the device.

Drawing with Claude only works when the app is opened on claude.ai. It asks the viewer once for permission and uses their Claude account. Opened as a plain file (`index.html`), 🔄 cycles through matching emoji instead.

**Routines and settings** (below the board):
- **This routine:** rename it, move it to another part of the day, choose **Shows on** (school days, weekends, both), change the order, start it over, or delete it.
- **Ready-made routines** for this part of the day and kind of day, **+ Blank routine**, and **Start the whole day over**.
- **Quick add** from about 120 library pictures.
- **Settings:** follow the clock, start fresh each morning, switch to Weekend automatically, taps change color, speech, celebration, tile size, voice speed.

Schedules and photos are saved only in this browser on this device.
