# Step by Step Schedule

A picture schedule for a 4-year-old learning school routines and transitions. It works like an AAC board or a Boardmaker strip: square picture tiles shown in order. Open `index.html` in any browser on a tablet, Chromebook or smartboard. Nothing to install.

## For the child
- **Tap a tile** and it says the step out loud (for example, "Hang up your backpack").
- Each tap also changes the outline color:
  - **Red**: not yet
  - **Yellow** (gently glowing): waiting for the child to follow through
  - **Green** with a check: done
- The current step is tagged **NOW** and the one after it **NEXT**.
- **First / Then** shows only two big tiles, the step now and the step after. Use it when a full strip is too much.
- **Read it** reads the whole schedule ("First… Next… Last…"), or "First… Then…" in First / Then view.
- When every tile is green, a star screen says "All done! Great job!"

## For the adult (tap **Adult**)
- Buttons under each tile set the color directly (✕ red, … yellow, ✓ green), move a tile earlier or later (◀ ▶), or remove it.
- **Ready-made schedules:** Arriving at school, Bathroom, Lunch, Recess, Going home.
- **Add a step** from 60+ school pictures in 7 groups: Arriving, Class, Self-care, Food, Places, Going home, Calm & wait.
- **Make your own step:** type the word, what it should say out loud, and pick a picture or **upload a photo**. Real photos of the child's own classroom, cubby or teacher often work best for autistic learners. Photos are cropped square.
- **Settings:** turn off "a tap changes the color" so the child's taps only speak and the adult controls the colors; turn speech or the celebration on and off; choose tile size and voice speed.
- **Start over** sets every tile back to red for the next time.

Schedules and photos are saved only in this browser on this device.
