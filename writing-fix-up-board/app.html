Teacher setup

Add a student's writing, check the words, then start editing.

1Add the writing

or click here and press Ctrl+V
After you add a photo, the app can read the handwriting for you. You can also type the writing in step 2.

Tip: fold over or cover the student's name before you take the photo.

2Check the words

Type exactly what the student wrote, mistakes and all, so they have something to fix. Leave a blank line between paragraphs.

Adds colored hint dots (C, U, P, S) under words that need a look. Tips never give the answer.

3Choose the labels

Students tag parts of their writing with these labels. They show up in the chart.

4Options
