Teacher setup

Add a student's writing, check the words, then start editing.

Pictures from your iPad

Pages you sent from another device appear here. Open them to use them in the lesson.

1Add the writing

Uses the camera on an iPad or phone. Take one picture for each page.
or click here and press Ctrl+V
Sends these pages to your claude.ai account so the smartboard can open them.
After you add a photo, the app can read the handwriting for you. You can also type the writing in step 2.

Tip: fold over or cover the student's name before you take the picture.

2Check the words

Type exactly what the student wrote, mistakes and all, so they have something to fix. Leave a blank line between paragraphs.

Adds colored hint dots (C, U, P, S) under words that need a look. Tips never give the answer.

3Choose goals and labels

Goals are what this lesson tracks. Every edit the student makes is saved as an attempt toward one of them, on whatever device they use.

Students tag parts of their writing with these labels. They show up in the chart.
