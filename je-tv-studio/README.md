# J.E. TV Studio

A smart board app for J.E., a Deaf-Blind student who loves YouTube. It looks like his own YouTube channel. Every "video" is an activity for one of his IEP goals, and every activity saves data as you go.

Open `index.html` in Chrome or Edge on the smart board. Nothing needs to be installed. The internet is only used to load the fonts. Press **⛶ FULL** for full screen.

## Accessibility controls (top bar, on every screen)
| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🔊 SOUND | Turns the computer voice and the "ding" sounds on or off |
| CC CAPTIONS | Turns large captions on or off. Captions show under the video, like YouTube's |
| Aa | Text size: BIG → BIGGER → HUGE → GIANT. The whole app scales |
| 🌗 | Colors: Dark (YouTube dark mode), Light, or **High contrast** (yellow on black) |
| ⛶ | Full screen. The player's own ⛶ button keeps the Coach Panel visible |

**Voice quality.** The app uses voices built into the browser. It ranks them and automatically picks the most human-sounding one on the device. In Studio Settings, voices are labeled ✨ Most natural, 👍 Good, or 🤖 Robotic, and there is a pitch slider. It speaks one sentence at a time with natural pauses, and the word highlighting follows the voice. For the best voice:
- **Windows board or PC:** use **Microsoft Edge**. It has free neural "Natural" voices (Ava, Andrew, Emma, Jenny).
- **iPad or Mac:** download a **Premium** or **Enhanced** voice in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.
- **Android or Chromebook:** use Chrome's "Google" voices.

**Studio Settings** has more: caption size, voice speed, which voice to use, channel name, picture, student initials, and the device name ("hearing aid", "cochlear implant", and so on).

Scores always show an icon and a word with the color: ✅ Independent · 🤝 With prompt · 🔁 Not yet · ➖ No response.

## Making activities
On **Home**, tap ✨ **New … video** to make a random activity for that goal. On a goal page, pick a specific activity type or 🎲 **Surprise me**. While a video is playing, use ✨ **New video like this** or 🎲 **Different … video**. Each new video uses different content.

### Goal 1 · 🗣️ Speak It (orally expressing a 1–2 sentence passage)
Passages match J.E.'s interests: YouTube, gaming, food, animals, sports, music, and school. Sight words are underlined.
- **📖 Read & Say**: the passage is read with word-by-word highlighting. Then "YOUR TURN!" appears.
- **🔁 Echo x3**: hear it and say it, three rounds in a row (repetition).
- **❓ Check It!**: listen, answer a picture question (check for understanding), then say the whole passage.
- **⚡ Word Power**: sight-word flash cards, then the whole passage.
- **Coach Panel**: tick 🔊 Audible / 💬 Intelligible / 📝 Whole passage, then score. The app suggests a score from your ticks. **🎙️ Record language sample** saves an audio file you can download as evidence. Recordings are not kept in the app.

### Goal 2 · 👯 Word Twins (synonyms and antonyms, T1 and T2 words)
28 built-in words (14 everyday T1 words, 14 academic T2 words), each with an example sentence.
- **👯 Word Twins**: pick the synonym. **🔄 Opposite Day**: pick the antonym.
- **🤔 Same or Opposite?**: two words. Are they the same or opposite?
- **🎤 Say Both**: say (or type on the board) a synonym, then an antonym. 👀 Show answers is available.
- Tap activities score themselves (correct on the first try = Independent). Afterwards, tap whether he **said it** or **wrote it**.

### Goal 3 · 🎧 Device Check (sound checks, advocating, troubleshooting, charging)
- **🎧 Sound Check**: a get-ready checklist, then the Ling 6 sounds (ah, oo, ee, sh, s, m), then "Is it working?" If the answer is no, he practices the help sentence.
- **🔧 Fix It!**: problem scenarios (no sound, whistling, low battery, wet, dropped…).
- **🙋 Speak Up!**: role-play asking for help with an advocacy script.
- **🔋 Charge Routine**: put the device-care steps in order.

## 📊 Data Tracker
- One card per goal shows **% Independent** against the **20% target**, the score breakdown, and the "with prompt or better" %.
- A **progress-by-day chart** shows all three goals with a dashed 20% goal line.
- **Daily Device Log**: a quick end-of-day form for Goal 3 data logs.
- **Add an observation**: record things that happened outside the app, with a language-sample notes field.
- The full data log can be filtered and edited. **Download CSV** opens in Excel or Google Sheets. **Print progress report** is also here.
- Fix a mis-tap during a video with the **💾 Saved: … Change** strip in the Coach Panel.
- Sight-word cards and quiz taps are saved as *practice* and don't count toward mastery.

**Data is saved only in this browser on this device.** Use **Backup** often (and **Restore** on another computer). Use initials, not full names.

## Adding your own content (Studio Settings)
- Custom passages, one per line: `passage | question | right answer | wrong answer | wrong answer`
- Custom vocabulary, one per line: `word | synonym, synonym | antonym, antonym | example sentence`
