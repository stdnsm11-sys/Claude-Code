# Storytime Studio

Turn your own sentences into an interactive picture book for young children, with your own companion video. Open `index.html` in any browser. There is nothing to install, and it works offline (the internet is only used for fonts).

## Make
1. **Your story:** type a title, an author and your sentences. Each sentence becomes a page, or choose **One page per line** to keep a few sentences together.
2. **Pictures appear by themselves.** They are simple cartoon stickers drawn by the page, chosen from the words:
   - **Characters:** cat, dog, bunny, bear, bird, duck, fish, frog, pig, owl, butterfly, turtle, mouse, lion, elephant, cow, sheep, penguin, dinosaur/dragon, plus people (mom, dad, grandma, grandpa, baby, boy, girl, teacher, "I"/"me").
   - **Names:** a name like *Mia* becomes a child who looks the same on every page. Write *"Pip the bunny"* or *"a cat named Tom"* to make a named animal.
   - **Things:** sun, moon, stars, cloud, tree, flower, house, car, boat, ball, cake, apple, balloon, heart (love/hug), rocket, umbrella, book, cookie, ice cream, present, kite, snowman.
   - **Places and weather:** beach/sea, snow/winter, forest, bedroom, outer space, night, sunset, rain, rainbow.
   - **Details:** numbers (*three fish*), colors (*a red balloon*), feelings (*sad*, *sleepy*, *wow*, *angry*, *!* for excited) and actions (*jump*, *dance*, *fly*, *swim*, *run*) change the faces and movement.
   - If a page names no character, the characters from the page before stay on screen.
3. **Fix any page:** remove a picture with ×, add one with **+ Add a picture**, or pick a different **Place**.
4. **Companion video:** choose a video file (saved in this browser) or paste a YouTube or .mp4 link. To match the book to the video, play the video, pause where a page starts and press **Use video time** on that page, or type the time (for example `1:05`).

## Read
- Big tappable words: tap a word to hear it. Words with a wavy underline make their picture jump.
- Tap a character to hear its sound (*Meow!*, *Quack!*) in a speech bubble. Tap the sky for sparkles.
- **Read to me** reads the page aloud and highlights each word. **Turn pages for me** reads the whole book.
- **Watch the video** opens it beside the book. If pages have start times, the pages turn by themselves while it plays, and turning a page jumps the video to that part.
- Swipe, arrow keys or the round buttons turn pages. **Full screen** works well on a tablet or smartboard.

## Saving
The book saves itself in this browser. **Save, share, start over** gives you a book code to copy to another device. The video file itself stays on the device where you added it.

`app.html` is the same page without the document wrapper (used for the hosted version).
